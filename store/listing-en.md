## Title

MGym

## Short description

Bench, squat and deadlift plans, workout diary, RPE and progress. No sign-up.

## Full description

MGym is a free strength training diary with offline planners for the bench press, squat and deadlift. Each lift has its own plan and workout history.

Build a program around your current performance, experience, age, recovery, available weekdays and target date. View warm-ups, working sets, weights, repetitions, rest periods and illustrated assistance exercises. Record actual results and RPE for the indicated main sets. Saving a completed session automatically adjusts future loads using verified results and RPE while preserving recorded sets and dates.

• Plan 1–4 training days per week, from two weeks to two years.
• Change your goal, target date and schedule while keeping recorded sessions.
• See a forecast once enough actual training data is available. Planned weights are never treated as achieved results.
• Delete an unwanted plan with a recovery backup while keeping your workout diary.
• Coordinate nearby squat and deadlift sessions across current programs.
• Save plan results to the diary without creating duplicates when editing.
• Track rest time, calendar, progress, records and working-set RPE.
• Keep data locally, back up the diary and plans separately as JSON, and export CSV.
• Use the built-in planner offline. No advertising or account required.
• Choose Russian, English, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Simplified Chinese or Kazakh in Settings.

The planner is designed for training without performance-enhancing drugs. Estimated weights and e1RM are approximate; reaching a target is not guaranteed. Supported ages are 14–100, with individual supervision required below 18 and above 85. Pain blocks automatic load progression.

MGym is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent diseases. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health and suitability for exercise.

All features are free. Any reward to the developer is entirely voluntary. There is no payment mechanism in the app. Made by an athlete as an enthusiasm project, free for everyone. Support: info@mg-trener.com. WhatsApp is also available in Settings.
